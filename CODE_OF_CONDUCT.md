# Code of Conduct

Karvon brings together developers and people who run terminals, from many
countries and languages. We want everyone to feel welcome here.

## Our standards

Do:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language;
- give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people;
- assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear;
- respect that people have different levels of experience and different working conditions.

Don't:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, including through sexualized language or imagery;
- make discriminatory jokes or comments about nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, age, disability or any other personal trait;
- publish other people's private information, such as addresses, phone numbers, passport numbers or vehicle data from a real terminal;
- keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other place where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer,
[Khan-ipak](https://github.com/Khan-ipak), using the
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Khan-ipak/karvon/security/advisories/new)
or any other private channel. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban someone who does.
